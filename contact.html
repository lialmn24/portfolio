How I work

Solving problems, one step at a time

Good projects start with a good conversation. Whether you bring a detailed brief or a rough idea, I’ll help shape it, keep you updated at every stage and focus on what matters most to the people using it.

First I get to know the goal, who it’s for and any limits we have, like deadlines or requirements. Then we agree on a plan so every decision later has a clear reason behind it.

Next I turn the plan into layouts and simple prototypes, so you can see and try the idea before it’s built. I care about how it looks and how it works, so it’s easy to use.

Then I build it with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and other tools from my course, keeping the code clean and testing on phones, tablets and desktops so it works for everyone.

Finishing isn’t the end. I take feedback from teachers, teammates and users, fix what’s not working and keep improving, because every project is a chance to learn.
