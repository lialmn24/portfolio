  1. 01

    The Work

    I’m a BSIT student at La Consolacion College Tanauan, building websites, Laravel systems and Flutter games. I like working where design and code meet: sketching how something should look, building it, then testing it until people can actually click, play and enjoy it. I care about it looking good, and just as much about it working properly.

  2. 02

    The Approach

    When I start a project, I think about the people who will use it and what gets in their way. That’s how my library and maintenance ticketing systems took shape. Because I build what I design, I can take a layout and turn it into a fast, working site or app, keep the code clean, and test it on phones, tablets and desktops.

  3. 03

    The Curiosity

    I keep learning outside class too: Cisco networking, HTML and JavaScript Essentials, and events like BITCON 2025. I enjoy game development, accessibility and interactive design. Whether I’m building a browser game, working with classmates, or taking a project from first idea to launch, I try to keep the process simple and the result focused on the people using it.
